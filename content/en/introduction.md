This site collects information about **international parental child abduction involving Poland** and organises the material into the main legal and practical topics covered by the author.

Start with [what parental child abduction means](/parental-child-abduction/) and [what to do if your child has been taken to Poland](/what-should-i-do/). You can also read about the [Hague Convention](/the-hague-convention/) and [child abduction law in Poland](/within-the-law/).

The information is provided by **Kancelaria Adwokacka Elżbieta Bansleben**, a Polish legal practice. It is general information and does not replace advice on the facts of an individual case.
