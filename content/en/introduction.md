If your child has been taken to Poland, kept in Poland after an agreed stay, or removed from Poland without your consent, the legal questions can become urgent very quickly. This site explains **international parental child abduction involving Poland**, the rules that may apply and the practical steps available to a parent.

Start with [what parental child abduction means](/parental-child-abduction/) and [what to do if your child has been taken to Poland](/what-should-i-do/). For international return cases, see the [Hague Convention](/the-hague-convention/) and the information on [child abduction law in Poland](/within-the-law/).

The information is provided by **Kancelaria Adwokacka Elżbieta Bansleben**, a Polish legal practice. It is general information and does not replace advice on the facts of an individual case.
